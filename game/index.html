THE MIDNIGHT VAULT

a three-job stealth heist
JOB 1/3 — THE GALLERY SCORE $0 LOOT 0/16 COINS ●●●
ALARM 0%
LIVES ●●● TIME 0:00 Objective: crack the VAULT, grab the loot, reach the EXIT.
How to play: move with WASD or arrow keys, or click a spot on the floor to walk there. Hold Shift to sneak — slower, but guards and cameras spot you half as fast. Press Q to toss a coin — the nearest guard walks over to investigate the clink ($250, at most 3 tosses per job). Stay out of the yellow vision cones and the blue camera sweeps; being seen fills the alarm meter. At 70% every guard gives chase; at 100% the building goes into lockdown. Grab the gold, cash, diamonds and art, crack the VAULT ($5,000), then slip out through the green EXIT. Three jobs: the museum, the bank, the penthouse. M mute  ·  P pause  ·  R restart the job.
